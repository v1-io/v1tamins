# Slides, diagrams, graphics, and documents

The three rules and the six steps still apply. This file changes what "what
exists", "a tasting", "the floor", and "shipping size" mean for each medium.

## Slides

- **What exists:** the deck's theme, master layouts, and earlier slides. Build
  inside them.
- **Tasting:** a title slide and one content slide per variant, in one file.
  Never the whole deck.
- **Floor:** one idea per slide, and the title states that idea as a sentence.
  Body text at 24 px or larger on a 1920 by 1080 slide. About 40 words at most
  outside the notes. One grid and one alignment across the deck. Contrast as in
  the main floor.
- **Shipping size:** full screen, and also the thumbnail strip, where the
  sequence has to make sense at a glance.

## Diagrams

- **What exists:** the project's other diagrams and their notation.
- **Tasting:** usually none. Clarity decides. Offer two layouts side by side
  only when the structure can honestly be drawn two ways.
- **Floor:** one reading direction. About nine elements in view; group the
  rest. One shape per kind of thing. Labels sit on or beside what they name.
  Colour carries meaning, and a label or legend repeats that meaning for anyone
  who cannot see the colour. Lines cross as little as the structure allows.
- **Shipping size:** the size it is embedded at, in light and dark themes when
  the host page has both.

Send a chart of quantities to `v1-reviewing-data-graphics` for integrity
review.

## Graphics and hero images

- **What exists:** brand colours, fonts, logo rules, and earlier graphics.
- **Tasting:** variants side by side in one file.
- **Floor:** one focal point. Text readable at the smallest size the graphic
  will be shown at. Clear margins that survive cropping. Real imagery or none;
  mark any stand-in image as a stand-in.
- **Shipping size:** every size and crop the destination uses.

## Documents and reports

Treat a report, a one-pager, or a long HTML page as an Understand surface. Hold
body lines to 45 to 80 characters, use a clear heading scale, and put the
conclusion where the reader starts. `v1-html-it` owns a standalone HTML
artifact's job, structure, and delivery; supply its visual decisions from this
skill.

## Outside this skill

Narrated animation belongs to `v1-explainer-video`. Native iOS and Android
platform conventions are not covered.
