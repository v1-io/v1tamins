Interview users by default before planning an explainer video, with a "just make it" option in every round and storyboard review before production. Resolve each production choice from the request, then standing preferences, then generic fallbacks. Ask whether the video should follow brand assets, help find them from guides, design files, websites, folders, or examples to imitate, and apply them without inventing missing brand details. Let other skills run `v1-interview-me` for their own choices. Scripted or headless callers that expect a finished video without questions must now say "just make it" or ask for no questions.
