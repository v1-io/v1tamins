Add `v1-taste-buds` to design, restyle, and critique what people see: UI, pages, components, slides, and diagrams. It reads the existing design first, offers on-screen variants and optional brand directions when the look is open, and helps choose what leads on a page. It uses supplied or generated imagery where it serves the design, checks the rendered page at shipping sizes, and records approved decisions in the project's design document.
